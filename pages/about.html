Two Businesses, One Standard of Service

Jetour International Ltd. brings together vehicle importation and agricultural operations under a single, dependable company.

Who Jetour International Ltd. Is

Jetour International Ltd. is a small, focused company built around two areas of work: sourcing and importing vehicles for customers who need them, and running agricultural activities including farming and livestock.

These are not two brands sharing an office. They are two working parts of the same company, run by people who understand that a vehicle enquiry and a livestock question need very different kinds of attention. What connects them is not the subject matter. It is how we handle each one: promptly, honestly, and without overpromising.

We are still a small company, and we are not pretending otherwise. What we can offer is direct communication and a team that actually follows up, rather than a large operation that loses track of individual requests.

How We Approach Both Sides

Running two different kinds of business under one name only works if neither side is treated as the "main" one. We do not let automotive enquiries wait because agriculture is busy, or the other way around. Each has its own attention, and each customer gets a direct line to the person actually handling their request rather than being passed around.

That also means being upfront when something is outside what we currently offer, instead of stretching the truth to close an enquiry. We would rather tell you clearly than leave you assuming.

Our Mission

To provide dependable vehicle sourcing and importation services, and to build a productive agricultural operation, both handled with honesty and care.

Our Vision

To grow into a trusted name in both vehicle importation and agriculture, known for reliability in each area we operate in, not just the one that happens to be more visible.

Core Values

The same four principles apply whether we are answering a vehicle enquiry or managing something on the farm.

01

Reliability

If we tell a customer we will follow up by a certain point, we follow up. Small as it sounds, this is where most of the trust in this kind of work is actually built.

02

Quality

We would rather take a little longer on a vehicle sourcing request or a livestock decision than rush it and leave a customer with something that does not fit what they asked for.

03

Integrity

When timelines shift or something does not go as planned, we say so directly instead of letting a customer find out on their own.

04

Commitment

Both sides of our business are long-term commitments. We are building them to still be here in years, not just until the next opportunity comes along.

About Jetour International Ltd.

Jetour International Ltd. operates across two areas: sourcing and importing vehicles, and running agricultural and livestock activities. Two different worlds, held together by one standard of service.

The automotive side exists to solve a practical problem: finding and importing the right vehicle without the customer having to manage every step themselves. The agricultural side is a separate, longer-horizon undertaking, built and grown alongside it. We do not try to make the two look identical. We just hold both to the same standard of follow-through.
